1. Executive Summary

With 50% of Initia’s fixed 1 Billion $INIT supply (500,000,000 INIT) allocated across Enshrined Liquidity (250M) and the Vested Interest Program (250M), Initia has built crypto's most sophisticated incentive alignment mechanism. However, there is currently zero unified transparency for stakers and rollup teams to track real-time gauge voting weights, bribe pools, or dual-yield staking APYs.

InitiaPulse is an open-source, non-custodial analytics terminal (the "DeFiLlama of Initia") that demystifies Enshrined Liquidity inflation ($r_a$) and tracks the 2-week VIP gauge voting cycles across all Minitias. We are requesting a $25,000 milestone-based grant from the Initia Foundation to maintain this infrastructure as a free, non-commercial public good for the entire Interwoven Economy.

The Problem for the Ecosystem

  • Opaque Staker Yields: Stakers cannot easily compare pure L1 staking (14.2%) vs. Enshrined LP staking (32.4%) vs. VIP gauge bribes.
  • Minitia Blindspots: Rollup teams lack a mathematical model to calculate how many votes they need to capture their share of the 538,461 INIT stage reward.
  • Fragmentation: Data is scattered across raw Cosmos RPC nodes, Minitswap pools, and multiple L2 sequencers.

The InitiaPulse Solution

  • Live Gauge Wars Leaderboard: Real-time tracking of votes, bribe pools, and projected INIT distributions per 2-week cycle with 40% cap warnings.
  • Enshrined LP Matrix: Real-time formula execution of $r_a = (w_a \cdot r_{total}) / \sum w_i$ with blended fee + staking APRs.
  • Minitia Bribe ROI Simulator: Interactive calculator for rollups to optimize bribe efficiency (demonstrating ~5.5x subsidy multiplier).
